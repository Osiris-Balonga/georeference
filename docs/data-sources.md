## Bundled factual reference

The default CLI reads a curated runtime asset, `reference/congo.json`, containing source-backed Republic of the Congo facts. It does not download GeoNames country lists to manufacture city or neighborhood coverage. A human coverage/source summary is generated by default; `--audit` includes the detailed source URLs, dates, locators and available revisions. Raw Gazette PDFs and unrelated personal/electoral records are not bundled.

The source layers are government electoral observations from 2022, municipal neighborhood tables observed in January 2024 and reproduced in a June 2024 government report, territorial laws from October/December 2024, and a national administrative/electoral registry from 2025. See [the reference guide](congo-reference.md) for exact URLs, dates, hierarchy, and ambiguity policy. Those publication dates are not a claim that every place was reverified in 2026.

Only records with an admissible named observation and documented parent enter a named-neighborhood output. A numeric quartier code without a verified name is a coverage gap, not permission to invent a label. Polling locations, schools, and venues in another source-table column are not automatically neighborhoods. Unclassified observations and conflicting spellings remain explicit in coverage/provenance rather than being silently merged.

## Rights and attribution

242Places's code and documentation are [MIT licensed](../LICENSE). Source facts keep their provenance and applicable source terms. Congo's [law 24-82 of July 7, 1982](https://www.sgg.cg/textes-officiels/lois/1982/congo-loi-1982-24.pdf), article 14 on page 4, excludes statutory, judicial, and administrative decision texts and official translations from that copyright law. The reference curates administrative facts from those instruments; it does not claim a blanket MIT license over government websites, maps, photographs, or unrelated reports.

Preserve source instrument/date/page/article and available SHA-256 revisions when redistributing a generated dataset. The municipal tables contribute factual names, codes, and parents only; no report prose, graphics, or general open-data license is republished or asserted. A checksum identifies source bytes or export integrity, not authorship or current geographic completeness. An outside source added later must have its own rights assessment; public readability alone is not an open-data license.

## Custom inputs

Custom inputs must meet the typed UUID Congo resource contract and retain their own sources/coverage. Source import helpers alone do not assign verified geographic kinds. If using the legacy GeoNames country-information importer as a low-level compatibility tool, its [official extract documentation](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt) specifies [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Keep GeoNames attribution for data taken from it. That optional country-only path is not the source of bundled quartiers or administrative reassignments.

No coordinates or boundary polygons are invented. Future geometry sources need independent coverage, precision, revision, and redistribution evidence. Keep actual source downloads and generated exports in ignored working directories; the curated factual runtime catalog is maintained separately from production exports.

Synthetic data belongs to automated tests only and is not the geographic catalog or a generated default fallback. Neither unavailable networks nor missing observations trigger fictional output.
